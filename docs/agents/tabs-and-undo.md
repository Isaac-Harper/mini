# Tabs, undo, splits and little windows

Detail for the tab model, tab undo, auto-archive, split view, drag and drop, tear-off and little windows. AGENTS.md has the one-line summaries.

## Tab model

- Tab ids are minted in Swift from one shared `TabIdentifierMint`, so ids are unique across spaces. Browsers are created lazily: restored tabs have no browser until first selected (or shown as the other side of a split).
- Tab kinds: pinned (`Tab.pinnedURL`, `TabStore.pinnedTabs`), saved (`Tab.savedURL` plus `savedTitle`, the tree under `TabStore.savedRootTabs`) and regular (`rootTabs`). Kind follows the tree: every tab in a saved root's subtree is saved, so moving a parent between sections changes its whole subtree (`setSaved(_:throughout:)`), and root-level siblings come from `siblings(of:)` by kind. New root tabs are always regular (a sibling that is saved or pinned puts them at the top of the regular list); new children of a saved tab are saved. `close` resets a saved tab like a pinned one: a fresh lazy Tab (new id) at its saved URL and title takes its place and keeps its children; `remove` is the real close. `treeRoots` is saved then regular, and session index paths, selection paths and `visibleTabs` (`VisibleTab.isSaved`) use that order. A split with a saved side is emitted in the saved section because saved rows come first.
- `Tab.swift`: tab node (observable) with favicon image, pinned URL, host view, browser state, `lastSelectedAt` (persisted) and `hasCrashed`.
- `TabStore.swift`: pinned tabs (flat) plus the tab tree, selection and tree operations; id minting; `TabSplit` and the split list (`makeSplit`, `unsplit`, `splitCandidate` = the most recently selected other unpinned tab). `visibleTabs` emits one row per split (carrying `split`) at whichever side comes first; the other side's row is skipped but its children still show. Closing a pinned tab swaps in a fresh unloaded Tab (new id) at its pinned URL. Duplicate Tab (`ShellController.duplicate`, Tabs menu Shift-Cmd-D and the row/tile context menus) opens a new sibling right after the original with its URL, title and favicon (a pinned original's copy goes to the top of the tree); navigation history is not copied.
- `SavedTabsSection.swift`: the sidebar's "Saved" label and divider (shown only when the section has rows or a drag can drop into it) and the dashed "Drop to Save" row with `SavedEndDropDelegate` (target `.savedEnd`, shown while a non-saved tab is dragged). Saved rows are dimmed while unloaded and unselected and show an `arrow.uturn.backward` button while `Tab.hasLeftSavedURL`; their context menu has Remove from Saved and Delete instead of Close Tab. Tabs > Save Tab (Cmd-D) toggles; the command bar marks saved tabs with `bookmark.fill`.

## Multi-select

- `TabStore.rowSelection` is a `RowSelection<MiniTabId>` (`Sources/MiniRules/RowSelection.swift`, unit tested): ordered ids in visible order (`visibleTabs` ids, so a split row is its leading tab), an anchor and a cursor. Shift-click selects the range from the anchor (else the active row) to the clicked row across nesting; Cmd-click toggles rows, starting from the active row; fewer than two rows clears it. `ShellController.clickRow` reads the click's modifiers; a plain click clears and selects. Pinned tiles are never part of it.
- A modified click focuses the sidebar without the keyboard ring (`SidebarKeyboardFocus.showsRing` false; any arrow, Delete or Escape shows it), so Shift-Up/Down extend from the focused row, Delete closes the group and Escape clears it. The sidebar resigning first responder (a click into the page) clears it, and `tabStoreDidChangeStructure` drops ids that are no longer visible.
- Group actions (`SidebarSelection.swift`, each one `performUndoable` step): close (Cmd-W, whose menu title becomes "Close N Tabs", and Delete), archive (removes them and adds archive entries; the entries stay after undo), pin, save, make regular, move or nest by dragging a selected row (relative order kept; dropping onto a selected row is refused), Open in Split View with exactly two, Copy Links (title line then URL line per tab, the shape `PastedLinks` reads, "Copied" toast). Split partners of selected split rows are included, and descendants of selected parents are dropped since the parent carries them. Tear-off is refused for a selected row while a group exists. The row context menu shows `GroupTabMenu` with counts when the row is in the group; single rows gained Archive Tab.

## Tab undo

- Every undoable tab action runs through `ShellController.performUndoable(name) { ... }` (TabUndo.swift), which snapshots the current store before and after and registers a restore on `ShellController.tabUndoManager` (target: the store; `levelsOfUndo` 100; a deleted space's actions are removed).
- It is a dedicated `UndoManager`, not the window's: a nil-target `undo:` that falls through the responder chain (field editor, page view, `NSWindow`) must never reach tab actions.
- `groupsByEvent` is off and every registration is its own explicit group: actions run from timers or drops outside an event otherwise merged into one group and one Cmd-Z undid two actions (seen in the test harness).
- `undoTabAction:` ignores a key event `pageUndoKeyPressed` already saw (`pageUndoKeyTimestamp`), so a Cmd-Z Chromium hands back to the menu is not performed twice. How Cmd-Z reaches tab undo versus the page is in shell-ui.md (Keys and menus).
- Undoable: close (including pinned reset and whole subtrees, and every group action), move by drag or Ctrl-Shift-Up/Down, indent, outdent, pin, unpin, split, unsplit, duplicate, paste-open, open link in split view, save, unsave, Remove from Saved, Delete, tear-off and dock. Not undoable: auto-archive, `browser_closed` from the page, space deletion, space color, history/site-data clears, collapse.
- `TabUndo.swift`: `TabArrangementSnapshot` (pinned, saved and regular tree nodes holding the `Tab` plus url, title, favicon, pinned URL, saved URL and title, collapse; splits; selection; a signature so no-op actions register nothing), `TabUndoRecord` (target, source, inverse) and the ShellController undo extension. Restore keeps live tabs, closes tabs in the source but not the target, revives target tabs that were closed as fresh lazy tabs (new id, its URL and title; navigation history is not restored; `tabUndoReplacements` maps old ids to the revived tab so redo/undo chains find it; the matching Reopen Closed Tab entry is dropped), and keeps tabs added since (not in either snapshot) at their old parent and index. `TabStore.restoreArrangement` applies it in one pass.

## Auto-archive and idle fade

- Auto-archive never closes the selected tab, pinned tabs or saved tabs (`ArchivePolicy.Candidate.isSaved`), and only closes a parent after all its children qualify; it is the only code path that closes tabs without user action besides `browser_closed`. The selected tab's split partner and audio-playing tabs are passed to `ArchivePolicy` as selected, so they are never archived.
- `TabArchive.swift`: archive entries carry an optional `spaceIdentifier` and reopen into that space when it still exists; `ArchivePolicy.tabsToArchive` (pure: unpinned, unsaved, unselected, idle 12 h, leaves before parents, a parent only when every child goes too) and `ArchiveStore` (archive.json, newest first, one entry per URL, cap 200). The command bar lists matches under "Archived" and reopens them as new tabs.
- `ArchivePolicy.idleOpacity` (pure) is the fade toward archive: 1 until 6 h idle, then linear to 0.5 at 12 h. The same pass (ShellController runs it at launch and every 10 min, no other timer) stores it in `Tab.idleFade` for regular tabs (1 for the selected tab, its split partner, playing, saved and pinned tabs); `TabStore.select` resets it to 1. `TabRowView` and split halves apply it to favicon and title except while selected or hovered (no animation, so hover restores at once).

## Split view

- `TabStore.splits` holds `TabSplit` pairs (leading, trailing, fraction) of unpinned tabs; `selectedTab` is always the focused side. `ShellController.refreshDisplayedTabs` is the only code that shows or hides host views: it shows the selected tab plus its split partner, hides everything else it showed before, and hands `WebAreaView` a `SplitArrangement`.
- A tab is in at most one split. Closing or pinning either side removes the split (`tabStoreDidChangeSplits` then re-arranges); the partner of the selected tab counts as selected for auto-archive.
- Split focus follows clicks through a local mouse-down monitor (hit-testing into the partner's host view), not CEF focus callbacks: `focus_requested` refuses focus for every tab but the selected one, so the partner page never focuses itself.
- The divider, its 320pt minimum and the focus border live in `WebAreaView` (shell-ui.md); its center snap and haptics in haptics.md.

## Sidebar drag and drop

- `SidebarDragging.swift`: `ShellController.sidebarDrag` (`SidebarDragModel`) holds the dragged tab and the current `Target` (`.row(id, .before/.after/.inside)` from the pointer's quarter of the row, `.pinned(index)`, `.end`, `.savedEnd`). Rows and split rows (`onDrag` + `TabRowDropDelegate`, the split row drags and targets its leading tab), pinned tiles and a dashed "Drop to Pin" tile shown while an unpinned tab is dragged (`PinnedDropDelegate`: reorder pins or pin at that index), and a two-row zone after the list (`TabListEndDropDelegate`, move to the end of the root list). `TabDropIndicator` draws the 2pt accent insertion line at the row's indent or the accent nest highlight. SwiftUI gives no drag-ended callback, so `beginDrag` polls `NSEvent.pressedMouseButtons` every 0.2s and clears the state on release.
- Tab moves are `TabStore.move(_:relativeTo:position:)` (refuses the tab itself, a descendant or a pinned target; a pinned tab dropped in the tree is unpinned), `moveToEnd`, `movePinned(_:to:)`, `pin(_:at:)`, and `move(_:by:)` for Tabs > Move Tab Up/Down (Ctrl-Shift-Up/Down, among siblings or among pins).

## Little windows

- `ShellController.littleWindows` (tab id -> controller) is the registry; `BrowserEvents.liveTab` looks there after the store, so every engine callback reaches little-window tabs. They are never in `TabStore`, session.json, the archive or Now Playing.
- Links stay in the window; `open_tab_requested` (Cmd-click) from one creates a main-window root tab; a popup with an opener gets its own little window. Permission and auth requests from little tabs are answered by remembered decisions or denied (the prompt cards only exist in the main window).
- Menu commands resolve their target from the key window: with a little window key, Cmd-W closes it, Cmd-L edits its address, and back/forward/reload/stop/zoom/devtools/PiP/Copy URL act on its tab (`commandBrowserTab`); find and Fill Password stay main-window only.
- `LittleWindowController.swift`: little window for outside links: 960x680 (clamped to the visible frame) centered on the screen holding the mouse, cascaded 24pt per open little window, standard titlebar with a unified compact NSToolbar (back, forward, centered host field that turns into an editable URL field on Cmd-L, Open in Mini). Its `Tab` is minted by `TabStore.makeDetachedTab` (shared id counter, not registered in the store) and its host view fills the window below `contentLayoutGuide`. `frame(hangingFrom:)` places a torn-off window under the drop point.
- AeroSpace (the tiling window manager on this Mac) tiles every new standard window, so a little window shows at 960x680 only until AeroSpace resizes it; its own frame code is correct (logged 960x681 before tiling).

## Promote, tear-off and dock

- Promote (Open in Mini, Cmd-O) reparents the live browser: the tab's host view (with CEF's NSView inside) moves from the little window into `webAreaView` and the same `Tab` object is `TabStore.adopt`ed as a root tab after the selected root, then selected. Verified: page JS state survives (a random number written at load and a running timer continue unchanged after the move, no reload). The little window then closes without `mini_browser_close`.
- Tear-off is the reverse and moves the same `Tab` the other way: `TabStore.release` takes it out of the store (a saved tab leaves a fresh lazy placeholder at its saved URL holding its children, a regular tab's children move up into its place; it is no longer saved), `ShellController.transferringTabs` makes `tabStore(didRemove:)` skip closing or hiding its browser, and its host view goes into a new `LittleWindowController` (a lazy tab gets its browser created there). Verified with the random-number and timer page: the number and the running counter survive tear-off, dock, and undo/redo of both, with no reload.
- Tear-off and dock register their own undo (`registerWindowTransfer`): undoing a tear-off docks the window back and restores the arrangement from before through the snapshot restore; undoing a dock tears it off again into its old frame.
- `TabTearOff.swift`: while a sidebar row is dragged, `SidebarDragModel` polls the pointer at 60 Hz (SwiftUI gives no drag position outside drop targets) and feeds `TabTearOffTracker`: past 40pt to the right of the sidebar, or outside the main window, a translucent `TearOffPreviewPanel` (borderless non-activating floating panel at 85% alpha, glass card with favicon and title, 240x170) hangs under the pointer, and releasing there calls `tearOff`, which opens a little window hanging from the drop point. Pinned tiles never tear off.
- Dock: a little window's `windowWillMove` starts `LittleWindowDockTracker` (60 Hz until the button is up): over the main sidebar of its own space it sets `SidebarDragModel.isDockingWindow` (a tinted outline over the sidebar) and the usual `target` (row quarter rule from `rowFrames`, `.end` elsewhere in the sidebar), and releasing docks it there. Rows of the main sidebar report their `.global` frames (hosting-view coordinates, flipped) into `SidebarDragModel.rowFrames` through `reportsSidebarRowFrame` (only where the `reportsSidebarRowFrames` environment value is set, so the peek sidebar never overwrites them). Docking in another space than the current one is not offered; Open in Mini still works for any little window.
- A tab drop that lands on the page itself still delivers the drag's `mini-tab:<id>` text to Chromium, as before.
